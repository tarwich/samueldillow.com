Botisimo was a creator-economy platform spanning chatbots, stream overlays,
loyalty, and monetization across Twitch, YouTube, DLive, Facebook, Discord, and
Slack.

The public case study is a reconstruction rather than a live product link. It
keeps the important product surfaces visible: analytics, creator controls,
chatbot command configuration, and a stream-frame design tool.
