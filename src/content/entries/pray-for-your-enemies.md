The Bible tells us to pray for our enemies. Jesus says it in [Matthew 5:43–48](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+5%3A43-48&version=NLT), Paul tells us to bless them in [Romans 12:14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+12%3A14&version=NLT), and even [Proverbs 25:21–22](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+25%3A21-22&version=NLT) tells us to feed them. But how do you pray for someone who makes you miserable? I've dealt with neighbors who go out of their way to make life difficult, coworkers who infuriate me, and even bosses who have made me want to change jobs.

At first in my prayer life, I prayed God would fix all my problems. Guess what? He didn't. Then I got upset.

## Vengeance Belongs to the Lord

I remembered that [Paul says not to take revenge](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+12%3A19&version=NLT), because vengeance belongs to the Lord. I was thrilled! All I have to do is pray for God to set them straight, right? I naively looked up the reference to see if there was some special incantation I would need to make. Seeing the cross-reference to Deuteronomy 32:35, I hurriedly flipped over and kept reading:

When I read that, it actually changed my feelings. If I'm to truly believe God will do what he said, I wouldn't wish that on my worst enemy. This sounds like a nightmare. I would much rather my enemy come to their senses and avoid such problems.

## Pray God Would Bless Them

Then I remembered that the Bible said to pray God would *bless* them! What if they're actually being such a pain because they HAVE pain in their life? What if my coworker is being a jerk because he's having trouble with his wife or kids at home? And what if it were possible, just possible, that if his life were better, he'd stop taking it out on everyone else?

I started praying for the people I resented. Praying God would give them better jobs, heal their families, help them prosper. To date, I haven't regretted it. I've had bosses come to me and ask how they can improve. I've seen people recover from significant problems and stop being difficult to work with. And I've had people leave and stop being my problem.

I've never had someone prosper and use that to step on me. And even if they did, I could just assume they still need more prayer.

I can't promise this is a magic bullet for you. For me, it's made a huge difference. Even when life doesn't magically improve, my attitude does, and that makes things more manageable.
